# Demo day

*[Previously,]({% link sept-5.md %}) the report was submitted and a final Saturday rehearsal put the demo video in the bag. This is the last entry.*

## The morning — slides and the mirror

Spent most of the day at home making slides and practicing in front of the mirror. A lot of practicing.

## The evening — campus

Went to campus in the evening for the demo. Walked through the slides, answered questions and pulled it off.

## That's a wrap

And that brings Picker-Bot to a close. Twelve weeks, four research questions, one robotic arm, one depth camera, one gripper that took five weeks longer than planned to behave, and a whole lot of entries in this blog.

If anyone wants to take this project forward and develop on it — please, feel free. The [code is on GitHub](https://github.com/MrRox1337/picker-bot/tree/pde4445-pickerbot-faseeh/pde4445-dev).
